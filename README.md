# Kaizen

Act as a senior Industrial Engineering expert in the garment manufacturing industry with hands-on experience in sewing line optimization and Kaizen implementation.

Create a professional PowerPoint presentation (slide-by-slide content) for top management of a garment factory, focusing on a Kaizen-based process improvement project in sewing lines.

Context:

 Current line efficiency: 63%

 Target (Phase 1 - Week 1): Improve to 65% (+2% improvement)

 Final Target: 70% efficiency

 Data source references: Internal Production Data (Mar 2026), IE Motion Study Reports, Maintenance Logs

Requirements:

 The presentation must be realistic, data-driven, and practical, not theoretical

 Include actual industrial engineering tools (Yamazumi chart, time study, line balancing, MODAPTS, TPM, etc.)

 Show before vs after comparisons, measurable impact, and logical reasoning

 Include tables, sample data, and realistic assumptions where needed

 Focus on convincing top management with clear ROI and operational benefits

Structure:

 Title Slide

 Business Problem & Current State (Efficiency = 63%)

 Kaizen Objective & Phase-wise Improvement Plan (63% → 70%)

 Week 1 Focus: Improvement from 63% → 65%

 Baseline Data Analysis (line efficiency, bottlenecks, machine utilization, motion waste)

Kaizen Pillars (Each should have separate slides with data & actions):

1. Bottleneck Elimination

 KPI: Improve bottleneck process efficiency from 75% → 80%

 Activities:

 Operator cycle time study

 Yamazumi chart for bottleneck identification

 Line balancing using Pro-SMV

 WIP buffer control

 Hourly pitch tracking

 Show sample Yamazumi data and before/after workload distribution

2. Motion Economy

 KPI: Reduce Non-Value-Added (NVA) motion by 30%

 Activities:

 Video analysis and both-hand chart

 MODAPTS-based motion optimization

 Workstation ergonomics improvement

 Gravity bins / material positioning

 Show example of motion reduction and time saved per operator

3. Machine Speed Optimization

 KPI: Increase machine utilization from 70% → 85%

 Activities:

 RPM/SPM measurement

 Machine setting optimization by fabric & stitch type (ISO 4915)

 Needle/feed calibration

 TPM implementation

 Include sample machine data and utilization improvement

Additional Slides:

 Week 1 Results (quantified impact contributing to +2% efficiency)

 Operator-level productivity improvement

 Line-level output comparison (before vs after)

 Challenges faced & corrective actions

 Financial/production impact (output increase, cost savings)

 Next Phase Plan (65% → 70%)

 Conclusion & Management Ask

Output Format:

 Provide slide titles + bullet content

 Include tables where needed

 Keep content concise, professional, and presentation-ready
